About Me

I’m Karan Tank, a Senior Security Engineer at Thales (Advanced Security Response Team). I specialize in incident response, threat detection, application defense, and penetration testing. My work spans web application testing, bot protection, DDoS emulation, and automating security testing to reduce manual effort.

I have hands-on experience in offensive security operations, conducting SQL injection, XSS, and RCE testing, building automated payload scripts, and authoring detailed vulnerability reports. I have trained and mentored interns, contributing to a stronger team and higher retention rates.

I hold certifications including OSCP+, SANS GPEN, eCPPTv3, SANS GCIH, SANS GSEC, SANS GFACT, CompTIA Security+, EC-Council CHFI, and constantly expand my knowledge through platforms like Proving Grounds, TryHackMe and HackTheBox.
