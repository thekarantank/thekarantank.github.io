06 / Operating system

Questions before tools.

This is the habit I bring to lab work and real-world security engineering alike.

“What are we protecting, how could it fail, and what would that failure look like?”

My starting prompts

  • What is the real attack surface?
  • Which route is likely, not merely possible?
  • What evidence would it leave behind?
  • How do we reduce the risk for real?
